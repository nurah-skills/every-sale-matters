Cards

When do we make a card?

Staff incentives earned: a new cash incentive entry or a higher weekly enrolment level makes a card with the new amount and the weekly total. Earnings already in the sheet aren't announced again. These are recorded earnings, not confirmation of payment.

Sales in one day: Bronze for the first sale, Silver for 3, Gold for 5, Diamond for 10, Platinum for 15 and Black for 20. The levels start again each day.

Beat your own best: more sales than your previous best day or week, or a month so far that beats your previous whole month. Matching a best doesn't count.

Cash: first payment of any size, then R1 000, R2 500, R5 000 and R10 000 in a day. Weekly milestones are R5 000, R10 000 and R20 000, and monthly milestones are R5 000, R10 000, R20 000 and R50 000. Only the highest milestone in each period gets a card.

Beat your own cash best: more cash than your previous best day or week, or a month so far that beats your previous whole-month cash.

Streaks: a sale on 5, 10 or 20 working days in a row. Weekends and public holidays don't break a streak.

Steady improvement: beat your August daily average on at least 4 recorded days in the same week. One card per person per week.

Thank you for an assist: a colleague nominates someone, and an admin approves a short thank-you. It doesn't add a sale or cash to anyone's totals.

How the queue works

Unsent wins for the same person on the same day share one card, and the biggest personal best leads.

If more sales come in after a card was sent, the card shows as “Figures changed” so an admin can check it. The card that was sent stays as it was.

Cash is the amount recorded under Paid on the sheet date. Cancelled rows, missing amounts and missing CRM IDs are left out, and repeated CRM IDs count once a month.
