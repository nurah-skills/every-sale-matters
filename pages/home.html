How your incentives work

The weekly enrolment incentive uses debit-order registrations. Below 25 it's R0. Only the highest level reached applies; levels aren't added together.

    Cash incentives are extra. The weekly total comes from the incentive sheet, including any adjustments, and “Paid” follows the sheet's checkbox. These are staff earnings, separate from cash received from students.

        How these numbers work

        A registration counts when a row has a salesperson's name and either a student name or CRM ID. “Confirmed” and “Docs Received” both count. “Cancelled” doesn't.

        The August average is August's registrations divided by its 20 recorded days, multiplied by the days in the period you're looking at. “Usually by now” scales that to how much of today's working day (08:00 to 17:00) has passed.

        Cash is the amount recorded under Paid on the sheet date. These are registrations and sheet amounts, not money received in the bank.
